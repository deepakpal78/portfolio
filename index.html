FAQ

How do we start and how do I pay?

Send a brief using the form below or message me on Fiverr. I reply with a scope and price. Payment goes through Fiverr, so your money is protected until you approve the delivery.

What do I need to give you?

Your logo, product photos and prices, page text if you have it, and any websites you like the look of. If you don't have text yet, I can add clear placeholder copy.

Can you fix or update my existing website?

Yes. Share the link or code and describe the problem. Small fixes are usually done within one to two days.

Will my site work on phones?

Every page is built mobile-first and tested on phone, tablet and desktop screen sizes before delivery.
